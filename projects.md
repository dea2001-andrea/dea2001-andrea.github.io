# Business Analytics Projects

This page highlights selected projects and analytical work completed during my **MS in Business Analytics at California State University San Marcos**.

## Quality Improvement and Defect Analysis

Analyzed manufacturing defect data to identify patterns involving:

- Suppliers
- Machines
- Operators
- Shifts
- Dates
- Defect types

Used **stratification and descriptive analytics** to investigate possible sources of process variation and determine which factors showed the strongest relationships with defects.

The analysis also considered possible special causes, measurement changes, and additional data needed before making causal conclusions.

### Skills Applied

- Descriptive analytics
- Process improvement
- Data stratification
- Root-cause investigation
- Business recommendations

## Association Rule Analysis

Applied association-rule techniques to evaluate relationships between items and behaviors in transactional data.

Metrics used included:

- Support
- Confidence
- Lift
- Leverage
- Conviction

The goal was to interpret the strength and usefulness of relationships from a business perspective rather than focusing only on the calculations.

### Skills Applied

- Data mining
- Association rules
- Pattern interpretation
- Business analytics
